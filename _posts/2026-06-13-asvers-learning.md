We’re happy to share our latest report, "Learning of Robot Safety Policies via Adversarial Synthetic Scenarios".

The work introduces a framework in which two AI teams work against each other: a Red Team generates challenging, 
potentially hazardous scenarios, while a Blue Team learns to improve robot safety policies in response. 
This adversarial approach aims to uncover high-risk edge cases that random simulations or manual testing might miss, 
helping pave the way for safer and more reliable Physical AI systems.

The report presents an ongoing research effort, outlining the problem formulation and proposed solution architecture.

🔗 Read the report on Arxiv: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05952](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05952)
