On 14 July 2026, Rostislav Yavorskiy, CTO of SafePi, presented our project at the 
4th International Conference on Automation and Mechatronics Engineering, Barcelona, Spain, [ICAME'26](https://www.icame.org/).

The presentation introduced SafePi’s Learning Data Framework for Robotics Physical Safety, outlining an approach to identifying 
potential hazards, generating synthetic training data, and improving AI-driven safety for robotics and physical AI systems.
