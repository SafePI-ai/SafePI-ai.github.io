We need machines that belong in our world.

They must be reliable like a mountain:
Unmoved by chaos. Silent in their strength.

Yet they must also be tender like a blossoming tree:
Alive to the child who runs beneath and soft enough to sway.
Their branches do not break the nest, they hold it.
